# Graphic Design Styles

1. **Minimalism**: Clean layouts, generous white space, and only the essential elements.
2. **Maximalism**: Bold, layered, and busy compositions packed with color, pattern, and texture.
3. **Futuristic**: Sleek, forward-looking visuals with sharp geometry, gradients, and a high-tech feel.
4. **Vector art**: Crisp, scalable illustrations built from clean lines, shapes, and flat color.
5. **Collage art**: Mixed cutouts, textures, and images layered together into one composition.
6. **Retro**: Nostalgic looks borrowed from past decades, with warm palettes and vintage typography.
7. **Cyberpunk**: Neon colors against dark, gritty backdrops, inspired by high-tech dystopian cities.
8. **Pop art**: Bold outlines, bright flat colors, and halftone dots drawn from comics and consumer culture.
9. **Glassmorphism**: Frosted, translucent panels with blur and subtle borders floating over colorful backgrounds.
10. **Clay style**: Soft, rounded 3D forms that look like hand-molded clay, with a playful, tactile feel.
11. **Pixel art**: Low-resolution, blocky graphics reminiscent of classic video games.
12. **Editorial**: Magazine-inspired layouts with strong typography, grids, and a clear visual hierarchy.
13. **Y2K**: Late-90s and early-2000s optimism, with chrome, glossy gradients, and techy shapes.
14. **Swiss design**: Strict grids, sans-serif type, and objective, highly legible compositions.
15. **Surreal design**: Dreamlike, impossible scenes that combine unexpected elements and distort reality.
16. **Bohemian**: Earthy, free-spirited visuals with organic patterns, rich textiles, and eclectic details.
17. **Victorian style**: Ornate, decorative designs with elaborate borders, flourishes, and classic serif type.
18. **Graffiti**: Expressive street-art lettering with bold outlines, drips, and spray-paint texture.
19. **Aurora**: Soft, glowing gradients that blend and shift like the northern lights.
20. **Handwritten**: Pen- or brush-drawn lettering that adds a personal, organic touch.
