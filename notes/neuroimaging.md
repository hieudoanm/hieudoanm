# **Neuroimaging**

1. **EEG (Electroencephalography)**

- Scalp EEG
- High-density EEG (HD-EEG)
- Intracranial EEG (iEEG)
  - Electrocorticography (ECoG)
  - Stereoelectroencephalography (sEEG)
- Event-related potentials (ERP)
- Quantitative EEG (qEEG)

2\. **MEG (Magnetoencephalography)**

- Conventional SQUID-MEG
- OPM-MEG (Optically Pumped Magnetometer MEG)
- Whole-head MEG
- On-scalp MEG

3\. **MRI (Magnetic Resonance Imaging)**

- Structural MRI (sMRI)
  - T1-weighted MRI
  - T2-weighted MRI
  - Proton Density (PD) MRI
  - Diffusion-weighted imaging (DWI)
  - Diffusion Tensor Imaging (DTI)
  - Diffusion Spectrum Imaging (DSI)
  - Neurite Orientation Dispersion and Density Imaging (NODDI)
- Functional MRI (fMRI)
  - Task-based fMRI
  - Resting-state fMRI (rs-fMRI)
  - Arterial Spin Labelling (ASL)
  - Real-time fMRI (rt-fMRI)
- Susceptibility-based MRI
  - Susceptibility-weighted imaging (SWI)
  - Quantitative Susceptibility Mapping (QSM)
- Magnetic Resonance Spectroscopy (MRS)
  - Proton MRS (¹H-MRS)
  - Functional MRS (fMRS)
- Functional connectivity MRI (fcMRI)

4\. **NIRS (Near-Infrared Spectroscopy)**

- fNIRS (Functional Near-Infrared Spectroscopy)
  - Continuous-wave fNIRS (CW-fNIRS)
  - Frequency-domain fNIRS (FD-fNIRS)
  - Time-domain fNIRS (TD-fNIRS)
  - High-density fNIRS (HD-fNIRS)
- Diffuse Optical Tomography (DOT)
- Diffuse Correlation Spectroscopy (DCS)

5\. **PET (Positron Emission Tomography)**

- FDG-PET (glucose metabolism)
- Amyloid PET
- Tau PET
- Dopamine receptor PET
- PET/MRI
- PET/CT

6\. **SPECT (Single-Photon Emission Computed Tomography)**

- Cerebral perfusion SPECT
- Dopamine transporter SPECT (DaT-SPECT)

7\. **CT (Computed Tomography)**

- Structural CT
- CT angiography (CTA)
- CT perfusion (CTP)

8\. **Ultrasound**

- Transcranial Doppler (TCD)
- Functional ultrasound (fUS)
- Transcranial focused ultrasound (tFUS)

9\. **Hybrid and Multimodal Imaging**

- EEG-fMRI
- EEG-fNIRS
- MEG-MRI
- PET-MRI
- PET-CT
