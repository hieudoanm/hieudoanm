# 20 Rules of AI Unslop Design

1. **Delete filler badges.** Remove badges that add no meaningful information.
2. **Drop redundant labels.** Eliminate unnecessary labels and repeated information.
3. **Unitalicise random words in headers.** Use italics intentionally, not as decoration.
4. **Use monospace only where necessary.** Reserve monospace for code, technical values, and data.
5. **Use a design system.** Establish consistent rules for typography, spacing, colours, and components.
6. **Show the actual product.** Use real product screenshots and interfaces instead of generic illustrations.
7. **Enlarge the main feature.** Give the most important element the visual space it deserves.
8. **Group with spacing.** Use whitespace to establish clear relationships between elements.
9. **Add specificity to your headline.** Make headlines descriptive, meaningful, and concrete.
10. **Emphasise the CTA.** Make the primary call to action visually distinct and obvious.
11. **Add an accent colour.** Introduce a deliberate accent colour to create visual hierarchy.
12. **Keep icons consistent.** Use a unified icon family, style, weight, and size.
13. **Remove unnecessary embellishments.** Eliminate decorative elements that do not serve a purpose.
14. **Follow the quarter-radius rule.** The inner quarter radius should equal the outer quarter radius minus the padding.
15. **Improve text contrast.** Ensure text remains readable against its background.
16. **Wrap headline text properly.** Control line breaks to create balanced, intentional typography.
17. **Align by eye, not just by pixels.** Prioritise optical alignment over mathematically perfect positioning.
18. **Use tabular numbers.** Align numerical values consistently, especially in dashboards and data-heavy interfaces.
19. **Add loading states.** Communicate progress instead of leaving users wondering what is happening.
20. **Animate useful feedback.** Use purposeful animations to communicate state changes and interactions.
